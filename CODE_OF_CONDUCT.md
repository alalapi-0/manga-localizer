# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for different viewpoints, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexual
language or imagery, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing another person's private information,
and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Maintainers will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through the private security contact
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers must
respect reporter privacy and may apply correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban according
to impact and context.
